[AIR MicroSoC DSP Architecture Overview]

Goal: predictable per-frame audio processing on micro SoCs. The real-time path uses
DMA-fed Q31 fixed-point CPU/DSP work; GPU compute is optional, asynchronous analysis
only. Codec transforms, quantization, and packetization must not depend on GPU timing.

Frame contract: 48 kHz mono/stereo with 7.5 ms or 10 ms frames. The runtime sums
critical-stage worst-case execution costs before accepting a frame. End-to-end wireless
latency is owned by the codec and transport profile and is not represented as sub-1 ms.

Codec contract: AIR packets are experimental and independent from LC3 / LE Audio BAP.
Production LC3 and LDAC paths are supplied through dedicated codec and transport adapters.
